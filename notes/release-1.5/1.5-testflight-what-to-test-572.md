Garage RAG turns the folders on your Mac into a private knowledge base your AI assistant can search over MCP. Everything runs on your Mac: its own database, its own embedding engine, no account and no cloud. Needs a Mac with Apple silicon.

WHAT'S IN 1.5
• A setup assistant walks you through access, sources, models and connecting your AI assistants.
• Redesigned Status, Sources, Models, Database and MCP Server pages.
• Garage lives in the menu bar: search, a one-line health summary, Stop, and Ask Garage, where the local model searches your files and answers with the documents it used.
• Update Everything scans, reads, indexes and gleans facts in one run. Only changed parts of a document are indexed again.
• Apple Mail and Messages can be indexed, with contact names instead of phone numbers and addresses (needs Full Disk Access; Contacts is asked once).
• HEIC and HEIF photos are read with on-device text recognition.
• Facts: a local model distills documents into short, sourced facts.
• Optional tips in About Garage…. They unlock nothing; TestFlight purchases are not charged.

GETTING STARTED (THE SANDBOX)
The App Store version runs in the macOS sandbox, so it can't read anything until you give it access:
1. In setup, choose Select Home Folder… and pick your home folder. That one grant covers Documents, Desktop, Downloads, iCloud Drive and the rest.
2. Optional: turn on Garage in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access, then quit and reopen Garage. Mail and Messages need this.
3. Pick starter sources or add any folder, then pick an embedding model. It downloads once and runs on the built-in engine.

WHAT TO TRY
• Run Update Everything and watch progress on Status and in the menu bar. Try Stop while it runs.
• Search for something you know is in your files, by meaning and by an exact word.
• Ask Garage a question from the menu bar and check the answer and its sources. Change the model on the Models page and ask again.
• Add Mail or Messages, allow Contacts, and check that names appear and sent mail counts as yours.
• Connect Claude Desktop or Claude Code on the MCP Server page and ask your assistant about your documents.
• On the Models page, load and unload models, and try a distillation model to glean facts.
• Database → Reset Database: Garage should relaunch into setup and bring your sources back.
• Quit and relaunch: sources, index and settings should all be there.
