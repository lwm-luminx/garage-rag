Garage RAG 1.5 turns the folders on your Mac into a private knowledge base your AI assistant can search over MCP. Everything runs on your Mac: its own database, its own embedding engine, no account and no cloud. This build needs a Mac with Apple silicon.

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.5
• Every page is redesigned: Status, Sources, Models, Database and MCP Server.
• Garage lives in the menu bar, with search, a one-line health summary and Stop for anything running.
• A setup assistant walks you through access, sources, models and connecting your AI assistants.
• Update Everything scans, reads, indexes and gleans facts in one run.
• Apple Mail and Messages can be indexed (needs Full Disk Access).
• HEIC and HEIF photos are read with on-device text recognition.
• Facts: a local model can distill documents into short, sourced facts.
• When a document changes, only the changed parts are indexed again.

GETTING STARTED (THE SANDBOX)
The App Store version runs in the macOS sandbox, so it can't read anything until you give it access:
1. In setup, choose Select Home Folder… and pick your home folder. That one grant covers Documents, Desktop, Downloads, iCloud Drive and the rest. Pick your startup disk instead if you want other disks too.
2. Optional: turn on Garage in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access, then quit and reopen Garage. Mail and Messages need this; without it they stay greyed out.
3. Pick one or more starter sources, or add any folder. Choosing a folder yourself also grants access to it.
4. Pick an embedding model. It downloads once and runs on the built-in engine.

WHAT TO TRY
• Add a source from the Sources page, then run Update Everything and watch progress on Status and in the menu bar.
• Search for something you know is in your files, by meaning and by an exact word.
• Add Mail or Messages after granting Full Disk Access, and check that sent mail counts as yours.
• Open the MCP Server page and connect Claude Desktop or Claude Code, then ask your assistant about your documents.
• On the Models page, load and unload models, and try a distillation model to glean facts.
• Quit and relaunch: your sources, index and settings should all be there.
• Try Stop while a scan is running.
