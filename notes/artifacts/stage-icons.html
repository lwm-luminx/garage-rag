Garage Stage Icons

Garage for macOS · pipeline stage icons

Scan, Read, Index, Distill

Four stage glyphs in one family: a 24-point grid, a 1.8-point round stroke and one filled accent each, so they sit next to SF Symbols in the menu-bar popover and the Status page without looking imported. Each is shown as a monochrome template and on the purple tile from the app icon work.

The app's own stage names are Scan, Ingest, Embed and Distill. "Read" here is Ingest (extract text, decide who wrote it) and "Index" is Embed (chunk and vectorise); the glyphs work under either set of labels.

Shipping them

  • As custom SF Symbols. Each glyph exports as an SVG into the SF Symbols app's template, which gives regular and semibold weights and the monochrome, hierarchical and multicolor rendering modes for free. Then they are Image with a symbol name in the asset catalog, and the stage trail, the Sources status column and the Facts section all take them where they use Image(systemName:) today.
  • Distill doubles as the Facts section icon in the sidebar (replacing the generic lightbulb) and as the badge for the Glean Facts action on a document.
  • Index and the catalog-drawer app-icon direction share a vocabulary, so if that direction wins, the two reinforce each other. If the roll-up door wins, Index still works: a card box is what you keep in a garage.
